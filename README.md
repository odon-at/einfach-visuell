# einfach visuell

Making visual communication with an AI tool easier and quicker.

Instead of describing what you mean, show it: drop a screenshot onto the canvas, drag icons onto it to mark and annotate what you're talking about, then capture the result with your system's screenshot tool and share it.

The app runs entirely in your browser — no account, no server, nothing is uploaded or stored.

## Usage

Open index.html in a browser, or visit the hosted version under [https://odon.at/tools/einfach-visuell/](https://odon.at/tools/einfach-visuell/). No build step or installation required.

1. Take a screenshot of whatever you want to talk about, and paste or drop it onto the canvas.
2. Drag icons onto it to point things out.
3. Take a screenshot of the result — ready to share.

Available in German and English.
